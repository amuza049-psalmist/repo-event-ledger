🎓 MY UNILIFE(UNZA)

Sign in or create an account for this browser. Use a long, unique password. If you forget it, you can reset this browser's account, but its saved data will be permanently deleted.

Resetting permanently deletes this browser's account, events, routine alarms, and stored files. It cannot recover them.

To add this app to your phone, open your browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app".

🎓 MY UNILIFE(UNZA)

Schedule academic and personal events, import Moodle/SIS calendars, and keep your files on this device.

    Alarms are not activated. Keep this page open and enable sound for browser alarms.

    My Daily Routine

    Set independent routine alarms. Choose a time and repeat once, daily, or weekly on a chosen day. Keep the app open for alarms; missed occurrences while it is closed are skipped.

    Routine alarms are separate from scheduled event alarms.

      Portal/import note: Sign in to Moodle or SIS, download your calendar as an .ics file, then import it below.

      📅 Event scheduler

      📥 Import calendar (.ics)

      Download an ICS calendar from Moodle or SIS, then select it here. Imported events are added to your scheduler.

      🗓️ Scheduled events

      Events stay saved on this device until you delete them. Past events are marked as missed with their scheduled time.

        📁 Course files & documents

        Files are encrypted and saved locally in this browser until you delete them. Tap a file to open it, or use its three-dot menu to open, download, share, or delete it. Logging out does not remove your schedules or files.
